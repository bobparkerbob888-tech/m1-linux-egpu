Private hardware constants have been replaced with PROVISION_PRIVATE_ID_N and
PROVISION_TARGET_UUID tokens. Machine-specific generated DT/guard data headers intentionally
stop compilation with #error. Do not substitute zeroes or remove identity checks.
An operator must collect and review the target's enclosure identities, GPU DSNs,
controller routes, resource windows, DART and MSI ownership, then generate the
headers/policy and run a fresh guarded cold-boot validation. There is no public
machine profile or generic identity bypass. This release is reviewable source,
not a ready-to-boot kernel or universal installer.
